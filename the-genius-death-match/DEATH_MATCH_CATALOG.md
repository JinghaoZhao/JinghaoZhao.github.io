## Detailed Game Specifications (All 20 Unique Death Matches)

### 1. Winning Streak — 연승게임 / 连胜游戏 (`winning-streak`)

- **Category & Tags:** `Mind Games` · Rock-Paper-Scissors, Social Diplomacy, Streak Optimization, Psychology
- **Appearances:** S1E1, S1E2, S1E5 (3× Death Match)
- **Difficulty:** ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) · **Est. Duration:** 5–8 min · **Hidden Info / Privacy Curtain:** Yes
- **Summary (EN):** Face a shared gauntlet of studio guests in Rock-Paper-Scissors to build the longest unbroken winning streak. Lobby guests, read tells, and manage your streak risk.
- **Summary (ZH):** 依次与观战嘉宾队列进行猜拳，争夺最长连胜纪录。在有限情报与游说机制下博弈，连胜中断仍须打完全程。

**Player Setup & 1v1 Web Adaptation**
- *TV Show Format:* 2 Death Match candidates vs. a queue of 8–11 safe (or returning eliminated) players in sequential Rock-Paper-Scissors.
- *Web Arcade Adaptation:* 1v1 (Human vs AI / Online P2P / Pass-and-Play) competing sequentially against a shared gauntlet of 9 AI Studio Guests. Each guest has a visible personality archetype (Loyal, Mercenary, Contrarian, Patterned) and hidden hand tendency; before each round, both candidates may spend limited Influence Tokens (or Garnet Bribes) to lobby guests or receive a partial tell before locking their RPS throws!

**Official Rules Specification**
- **Setup:** A shared ordered queue of 9 Studio Guests is revealed (with visible traits & prior tendency hints). Both candidates start with 0 current streak, 0 max streak, and 3 Lobby/Intel tokens.
- **Components:** 9 Guest Portraits in ordered gauntlet; 3 RPS Cards (Rock, Paper, Scissors) per player; 3 Intel/Lobby tokens per player; dual Streak Trackers.
- **Turn Structure:**
  1. Pre-Match Lobby Phase (3 actions): Each candidate secretly assigns their 3 Lobby tokens to guests (e.g., 'Request Throw Reveal' or 'Sway Loyalty'). Guests react based on archetype and both players' bids.
  1. Gauntlet Rounds 1–9: For each guest in order, both candidates independently lock their Rock/Paper/Scissors play against that guest's locked throw (each guest throws one fixed hand for Player A and one for Player B—or in S1 TV faithful mode, each guest picks a response per candidate influenced by pre-match diplomacy).
  1. Resolution: Win = current streak +1 (updates max streak if higher). Draw or Loss = current streak resets to 0. Both players MUST complete all 9 matches even if their streak breaks.
  1. Bonus Tension: After round 5, a mid-gauntlet 'Re-Order / Scout' check reveals the opponent's current max streak so trailing players can take calculated risks.
- **Hidden vs. Public Information:** Public: Guest order, guest archetypes, completed match results, both players' current & best streaks. Hidden: Lobby token targets, private guest intel tells, and locked RPS choice before reveal.
- **Scoring & Penalties:** Score = Longest consecutive WIN streak achieved across the 9-guest gauntlet (Draws break streaks just like Losses).
- **Win Conditions:** Higher longest consecutive winning streak after all 9 guests are played.
- **Tie-Breakers:** 1) Total wins across all 9 matches; 2) Fewest losses (more draws); 3) Sudden-death RPS overtime against a neutral Dealer Bot until one player wins a round while the other does not.
- **Season Variants:** S1E1: random guest order. S1E2: safe players chose shared order. S1E5: 4 eliminated guests returned in fixed order. Default web mode uses 9 guests with visible traits + 3 Lobby tokens.

**AI Strategy Blueprint (Easy / Genius / Grand Final)**
- *Easy:* Distributes Lobby tokens randomly and plays mostly uniform RPS with mild reaction to guest hints.
- *Genius:* Clusters Lobby tokens on consecutive middle guests (positions 3–6) to maximize expected longest contiguous streak rather than total wins, using Bayesian inference on guest archetypes.
- *Grand Final:* Computes exact dynamic-programming streak expectation, aggressively contests overlapping high-value streak clusters when trailing, and adapts counter-throws to guest loyalty shifts.

**Visual Motif & Centerpiece UI**
- *Centerpiece:* Neon Gauntlet Runway of 9 illuminated Studio Guest cards with dual glowing crimson/gold streak combo chains.
- *Accent Color:* `#FF3B5C` · *SVG Emblem:* Three interlocked obsidian RPS crests encircled by a burning streak lightning ring.

**Notable Broadcast Matchups**
- **S1E1** (Kim Min-seo vs Lee Jun-seok → Winner: *Kim Min-seo*): First-ever Death Match; social lobbying decided streak chains.
- **S1E2** (Cha Yu-ram vs Kim Min-seo → Winner: *Cha Yu-ram*): Safe players coordinated the gauntlet order to target streaks.
- **S1E5** (Cha Yu-ram vs Choi Jung-moon → Winner: *Cha Yu-ram*): Eliminated guests returned as wild-card RPS opponents.

### 2. Tactical Yutnori — 전략 윷놀이 / 战略掷柶 (`tactical-yutnori`)

- **Category & Tags:** `Board Strategy` · Yutnori, Simultaneous Choice, Simultaneous Bluffing, 2v2 Team
- **Appearances:** S1E3, S1E4, S1E10, S4E2 (4× Death Match)
- **Difficulty:** ★★★★☆ (4/5) · **Est. Duration:** 10–15 min · **Hidden Info / Privacy Curtain:** Yes
- **Summary (EN):** Zero-luck strategic Korean Yutnori: all 4 players secretly pick one of two Yut sticks (Flat vs Round) simultaneously—meaning you control half the throw and must outread your rival's half!
- **Summary (ZH):** 零运气的心理博弈掷柶：每方由主将与搭档组成，4人同时秘密决定投出平面或弧面柶木，4根合成步数（猪必为倒退猪！），先让主将2枚棋子绕完29格棋盘冲线者胜。

**Player Setup & 1v1 Web Adaptation**
- *TV Show Format:* 2v2: Each Death Match candidate selects 1 teammate. All 4 players hold 2 sticks (1 Marked/Back, 1 Blank/Front) and 2 Mal pieces, but only the Candidate's 2 Mals count toward victory.
- *Web Arcade Adaptation:* 1v1 (Human vs AI / Online P2P / Pass-and-Play) where each side commands a 2-character Duo (Candidate + Partner). On every turn, BOTH teams secretly choose 2 sticks (1 for Candidate, 1 for Partner), combining all 4 chosen sticks into the deterministic Yut throw! Each side moves its own 2 Candidate Mals + 2 Partner Support Mals (which can stack, block, and capture enemy Mals).

**Official Rules Specification**
- **Setup:** Standard 29-node Korean Yutnori board (20 outer perimeter stations + 9 interior diagonal shortcut stations including center). Each side has 2 Candidate Mals (gold-rimmed) and 2 Partner Mals (silver-rimmed) starting off-board at Start (node 0).
- **Components:** 29-node traditional Yutnori board; 4 sticks total (each of the 4 characters holds 1 Flat/Marked stick [F] and 1 Round/Blank stick [R]); 8 Mals (4 per side: 2 Candidate, 2 Partner).
- **Turn Structure:**
  1. Active Team Turn: The active team (Candidate + Partner) and the defending team (Opponent + Partner) BOTH simultaneously and secretly select 2 sticks (0, 1, or 2 Flat sticks each; equivalently total Flat count F_team ∈ {0, 1, 2}).
  1. Stick Reveal & Result (Total Flat sticks F = F_active + F_defending ∈ {0..4}):
    • F = 1 (3 Round, 1 Flat) → Back-Do (뒷도 / 빽도): move -1 step! (In Tactical Yutnori, ALL Do throws are Back-Do!)
    • F = 2 (2 Round, 2 Flat) → Gae (개): move +2 steps.
    • F = 3 (1 Round, 3 Flat) → Geol (걸): move +3 steps.
    • F = 4 (0 Round, 4 Flat) → Yut (윷): move +4 steps + BONUS THROW.
    • F = 0 (4 Round, 0 Flat) → Mo (모): move +5 steps + BONUS THROW.
