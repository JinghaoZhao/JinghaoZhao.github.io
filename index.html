The collaboration brings dedicated bare metal compute and deterministic networking to financial markets—combining the precision of physical infrastructure with the flexibility of the cloud.

02 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Systems thinker.
Curious by design.

I build at the intersection of cloud infrastructure, networking, and real-world systems.

Today, that means GCE Bare Metal at Google. Before that, I received my Ph.D. from the University of California, Los Angeles, advised by Prof. Songwu Lu, and my B.S. in Electronic Engineering from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

My research spans networked systems, network security, edge and cloud computing, and mobile networks. A common thread: understanding how systems behave, then making them work better.
